FLP Subchapter Elections 2026: BSCS Students Step Into Leadership

Mishca C. Alvarez
August 19, 2026

BSCS students step forward as emerging leaders, taking key roles in the 2026 FLP Subchapter Elections and embracing a new chapter of service, responsibility, and student leadership.

The Future Leaders of the Philippines (FLP) Subchapter Elections were held today, August 19, 2026, at Capiz State University – Mambusao Satellite College. The event brought together students from different year levels as they participated in the election of new officers, with several BS Computer Science (BSCS) students stepping forward to take on leadership roles in their respective FLP Subchapters.

During the election, different candidates ran for positions in the FLP Subchapter. The candidates presented their leadership qualities, ideas, and commitment to contributing to the growth and success of their respective subchapter.
