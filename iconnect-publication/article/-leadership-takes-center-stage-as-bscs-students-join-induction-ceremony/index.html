Leadership Takes Center Stage as BSCS Students Join Induction Ceremony

iConnect Publication
September 16, 2026

From codes to campus leadership, BSCS students step forward as newly inducted officers, with Trexy Heredero taking the helm of the Supreme Student Council.

MAMBUSAO, CAPIZ — Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) student leaders formally took their oath of office as officers of various student organizations during the recently concluded Induction Ceremony of Student Officers at Capiz State University – Mambusao Satellite College.

The ceremony gathered student leaders from different academic departments and campus organizations, marking the formal assumption of their leadership roles and responsibilities for the academic year.

Among those inducted were BSCS students serving in various organizations, including the Computer Science Society (CSS), Society of Arts and Students (SocArts), Senior Class Organization, Kalingawan Performing Arts Group (KalPAG), Himig Kalingawan, Ecovision Society, DRRM H.E.L.P.E.R, Red Cross Youth Council, Preferential-Options for Welfare Development and Inclusivity (PWEDI), The Grantees, and CM – Gender and Development Advocacy.

BSCS students also assumed leadership roles in the Future Leaders of the Philippines (FLP) through its chapter and sub-chapter organizations, further extending their participation in student leadership across the campus.

During the ceremony, Dr. Jelly L. Paredes, BSCS Program Chairperson, administered the induction of BSCS homeroom officers, while designated university officials led the induction of officers from the different student organizations.

A significant part of the program was the turnover of Supreme Student Council (SSC) responsibilities, documents, and funds from outgoing SSC President Ms. Janela Ponce to incoming SSC President Ms. Trexy Heredero, who is also a BSCS student. The turnover formally marked the transition of the council's leadership to the newly installed administration.

Heredero's assumption of the SSC presidency further highlighted the presence of BSCS students in campus-wide leadership, extending their involvement beyond the department and into the university's central student governing body.

Beyond the ceremonial oath, the induction signified a commitment to represent fellow students, uphold the responsibilities of their respective offices, and contribute to programs and initiatives for the campus community.

The involvement of BSCS students across numerous organizations reflects a community whose participation extends beyond codes, computers, and classrooms. From student governance and leadership to arts and culture, disaster preparedness, humanitarian service, inclusivity, and student welfare, BSCS students continue to take part in different areas of campus life.

With their oaths taken and a new SSC leadership formally receiving its responsibilities, the newly inducted officers now begin the work that follows the titles they carry.

From codes to causes, from classrooms to campus leadership—BSCS students step forward, ready to connect, serve, and lead.
