A New Network Is Forming — and You Might Be the Next Link

Prof. Art Jayson L. Osuyos
August 12, 2026

iConnect Publication opens applications for A.Y. 2026–2027, inviting BSCS students to find their voice, build their craft, and become the next link in a growing network of stories, ideas, and connections.

With a new academic year comes a new generation of voices ready to tell stories, share ideas, and connect the BSCS community. The publication is calling on passionate BSCS students who are ready to step forward, explore their creativity, and become part of a growing network built around communication, collaboration, and storytelling.

More than a student organization, iConnect serves as a space where students can find their voice, build their craft, and connect with fellow creatives and communicators. Through stories and creative work, its members can help amplify student experiences, highlight achievements, and strengthen connections within the BSCS community.

Joining iConnect is also an opportunity to learn beyond the classroom. Members can develop communication, writing, design, multimedia, teamwork, and leadership skills while contributing to a publication that represents the voices and experiences of BSCS students.

As the network begins to form, every student has the opportunity to become part of the connection. Every story is a link. Every voice is a connection. And every student has a story worth telling.

Applications for A.Y. 2026–2027 are now open to qualified BSCS students. Interested applicants may submit their application through the official Google Form or scan the QR code provided in the recruitment material.

Be the voice. Be the connection. Be the next link. 🔗
