Back To Campus, Forward To What's Next

August 10, 2026

The BSCS community of Capiz State University–Mambusao Satellite College welcomed A.Y. 2026–2027 as returning students, transferees, and first-year students joined the first flag ceremony and Pag-Abi-abi welcome program. Together with faculty, staff, and students from other academic programs, they marked the beginning of a new academic chapter filled with learning, opportunities, and shared experiences.

MAMBUSAO, CAPIZ — A new academic year began not simply with the opening of classes, but with a gathering that brought the Capiz State University–Mambusao Satellite College community together.

Returning students, transferees, and first-year students of the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) joined the first flag ceremony of Academic Year 2026–2027, marking the start of another year of learning, challenges, and opportunities.
For many students, the ceremony was more than a formal opening. It was their first step into a new academic chapter—one that would demand resilience, growth, and a renewed commitment to their goals.

The BSCS students stood alongside the faculty and staff of CapSU–Mambusao Satellite College, as well as students and members of the other academic programs: Bachelor of Arts in English Language, Bachelor of Science in Office Administration, and Bachelor of Science in Food Technology.

Following the flag ceremony, the campus continued the celebration through Pag-Abi-abi, a welcoming program prepared for CapSUnians for A.Y. 2026–2027. The activity served as an opportunity for students, faculty, and staff to come together, and welcome both familiar faces and those taking their first steps into the CapSU community.
For returning students, it was a reunion with a community they already knew. For transferees, it was the beginning of finding their place in a new environment. And for first-year students, it was the moment they officially stepped into a larger community—one that would soon become part of their college journey.

Across programs and year levels, the gathering reflected one message: 
"a new academic year is not only about starting classes; it is about starting together."
As A.Y. 2026–2027 unfolds, the CapSU–Mambusao community faces another year of possibilities. With students ready to learn, faculty and staff ready to guide, and programs united in one academic community, the first day has already set the tone for what lies ahead.

The year has begun. The community is ready. And a new generation of CapSUnians is ready to make its mark.
