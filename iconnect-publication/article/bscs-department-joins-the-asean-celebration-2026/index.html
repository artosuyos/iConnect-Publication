BSCS Department Joins the ASEAN Celebration 2026

August 25, 2026

The BSCS Department proudly joined the ASEAN celebration, embracing the diversity of Southeast Asian cultures while strengthening unity, collaboration, and community within CapSU Mambusao.

UNITED IN DIVERSITY: BSCS CELEBRATES THE ASEAN SPIRIT

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) Department of Capiz State University–Mambusao Satellite College proudly joined the celebration of ASEAN, standing alongside the different programs of the College in honoring the rich cultures, traditions, and shared spirit of Southeast Asia.
The celebration brought together students, faculty, and personnel in a colorful display of unity and cultural pride. For the BSCS Department, the occasion was an opportunity to look beyond the world of computing and recognize the importance of culture, collaboration, and community in building a connected future.

Adding a special highlight to the celebration, 𝗕𝗦𝗖𝗦 𝗖𝗵𝗮𝗶𝗿𝗽𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗼𝗻 𝗗𝗿. 𝗝𝗲𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗟. 𝗣𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗱𝗲𝘀 was recognized as 𝗕𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗶𝗻 𝗔𝗦𝗘𝗔𝗡 𝗔𝘁𝘁𝗶𝗿𝗲 – 𝗙𝗮𝗰𝘂𝗹𝘁𝘆, earning distinction for her elegant and meaningful representation of ASEAN culture through her attire.

Her recognition reflects not only creativity and cultural appreciation but also the BSCS Department's active participation in celebrating diversity and strengthening the spirit of unity within the University community.

Through the celebration, the BSCS Department continues to embody a simple but powerful message: different cultures, different perspectives, but one shared spirit—connected as one ASEAN community.
