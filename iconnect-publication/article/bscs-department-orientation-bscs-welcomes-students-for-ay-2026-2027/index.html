BSCS Department Orientation | BSCS Welcomes Students for AY 2026-2027

Mhyrien Claire Faceronda
August 20, 2026

The BSCS Department welcomed A.Y. 2026–2027 through its department orientation, bringing students and faculty together for academic guidance, community building, and the introduction of newly elected Computer Science Society officers.

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) Department conducted its department orientation on August 20, 2026, at the CAPSU Mambusao Satellite College Gym, bringing together students and faculty members as they prepare for the new academic year.

The program was hosted by Prof. Art Jayson Osuyos, who guided the flow of the activity and facilitated the different parts of the orientation.

The program began with a warm welcome and an inspirational message from BSCS Program Chair Dr. Jelly Paredes, who emphasized the department’s commitment to quality education, innovation, and academic excellence.

Following the opening message, Prof. Olga Llanera introduced the faculty and staff members of the BSCS Department, allowing students to recognize the individuals who will guide and support them throughout the academic year.

Prof. Leo B. Pula then discussed the rules and regulations of the Computer Laboratory, covering proper conduct, cleanliness, responsible use of equipment and software, internet usage, account security, and emergency procedures. Students were reminded to use the laboratory responsibly and help maintain a safe, orderly, and productive environment for everyone.

Meanwhile, Dr. Judith Vista presented the BSCS curriculum roadmap, providing students with an overview of the academic focus at each year level, from building foundational knowledge in the first year to developing core, advanced, and professional skills in the subsequent years. The discussion also emphasized hands-on learning, career opportunities, industry-relevant skills, and portfolio development.
