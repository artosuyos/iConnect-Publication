BSCS Students Join S.A.F.E. Program on Safety and Preparedness

Mhyrien Claire Faceronda
September 3, 2026

BSCS students, together with students from other programs, joined the S.A.F.E. Program to strengthen awareness and practical skills in safety, disaster preparedness, well-being, and responsible decision-making.

The S.A.F.E. (Safe and Secure Academic Facilities) Program was held on September 3, 2026, at 1:30 p.m. as part of the celebration of National Disaster Resilience Month. Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) students, together with students from other academic programs, participated in the activity, bringing the student community together for discussions and activities centered on safety, preparedness, well-being, and personal growth.

Hosted by Dr. June Rey Garbosa, the program opened with welcoming remarks, followed by a series of discussions addressing topics relevant to students and the wider school community.

One of the sessions discussed the different forms of bullying, including physical, verbal, social or relational, and cyberbullying. This was followed by a discussion on adolescence, highlighting the experiences and challenges young people may encounter during this important stage of life.

The program also featured a session on fire prevention and suppression awareness, where students learned about the importance of practicing fire safety and being prepared for fire-related emergencies. Participants were reminded to make fire safety a part of their everyday lives.

Other discussions focused on Children in Conflict with the Law (CICL) and Children at Risk (CAR), as well as mental health and well-being, emphasizing awareness, responsible decision-making, and the importance of caring for oneself and others.

For the final session, participants were introduced to life-saving skills under Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM). A demonstration was conducted, followed by a hands-on activity that gave BSCS students and participants from other academic programs the opportunity to practice the demonstrated skills themselves.

The activity concluded with a group photo featuring the speakers and participants. Overall, the S.A.F.E. Program brought together students from different academic programs and equipped them with useful knowledge and practical skills to help them stay safe, care for their well-being, respect others, and become more responsible and prepared individuals both inside and outside the school.
