Forging Future Innovators: Meet The Minds Behind The BSCS Department

Prof. Art Jayson L. Osuyos
August 16, 2026

Meet the dedicated faculty and staff of the BSCS Department who inspire, guide, and empower students to become future-ready innovators, technology creators, and catalysts for change.

MAMBUSAO, CAPIZ — Behind every line of code, every project completed, and every innovation pursued by a BSCS student is a mentor who helps turn ideas into possibilities.

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) Department of Capiz State University – Mambusao Satellite College is driven by a dedicated team of faculty and staff who play a vital role in shaping students into capable, creative, and future-ready computing professionals.

More than educators, the department’s faculty members serve as mentors, guides, and catalysts for growth. Through instruction, mentorship, and hands-on learning, they challenge students to think critically, solve complex problems, embrace emerging technologies, and transform ideas into meaningful solutions.

The strength of the BSCS Department lies not only in the curriculum it delivers, but also in the people who bring that learning to life. With their knowledge, experience, commitment, and passion for education, the faculty and staff continue to create an environment where curiosity is encouraged, innovation is nurtured, and excellence is pursued.

As technology continues to reshape the world, the role of educators becomes even more significant. The BSCS faculty stand at the forefront of this transformation—equipping students not simply to use technology, but to understand it, build with it, and use it to create solutions that can make a difference.

Every student who learns, every idea that takes shape, and every innovation that emerges is part of a larger mission:

" to prepare the next generation of computing professionals, innovators, and technology leaders."

The faces featured in this faculty and staff showcase represent the people behind that mission—the individuals who continue to teach, inspire, challenge, and empower the BSCS community.

Meet the minds behind the mission. Meet the people who power the BSCS Department and help shape the future of computing.
