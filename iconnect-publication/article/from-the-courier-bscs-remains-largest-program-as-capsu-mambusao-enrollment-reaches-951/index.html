FROM THE COURIER | BSCS remains largest program as CAPSU-Mambusao enrollment reaches 951

The Courier Publication
August 20, 2026

Computer Science continues to lead enrollment at Capiz State University–Mambusao Satellite College, with 519 BSCS students comprising more than half of the campus’ 951 enrollees for the first semester of AY 2026–2027. Original Story | Rigel Kent D. Delfin, The Courier

Computer Science continues to make up the largest share of the student population at Capiz State University–Mambusao Satellite College.

According to an enrollment report featured by The Courier, the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science recorded 519 students for the first semester of Academic Year 2026–2027—approximately 54.6% of the campus’ total enrollment of 951 students.

BSCS also posted the highest enrollment across all year levels, with 157 first-year, 129 second-year, 121 third-year, and 112 fourth-year students.

The figures place BSCS at more than three times the enrollment of the next-largest academic programs on campus, further highlighting the program’s significant presence within the Mambusao Satellite College community.
