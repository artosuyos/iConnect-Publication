Publication Identity

iConnect Mission & Vision

The guiding purpose, aspirational direction, and foundational principles that shape every story, feature, and creative output published under the iConnect banner.

"
Vision
To Be the Leading Digital Voice of the BSCS Community

iConnect envisions itself as the foremost digital publication platform that empowers Computer Science students to articulate ideas, document breakthroughs, and amplify authentic voices — fostering a generation of technically skilled and socially conscious communicators who bridge technology and journalism.

"
Mission
To Connect, Inform, Inspire, and Innovate

iConnect is committed to delivering accurate, creative, and technologically forward journalism that reflects the dynamic culture of the BSCS Department. We provide an open platform where students, educators, and the wider community can share knowledge, explore ideas, and celebrate the intersection of computer science and human expression — without barriers, without boundaries.

Publication Goals
What We Strive For
01 — Accuracy

Maintain the highest standard of factual, well-researched, and balanced reporting in every article.

02 — Creativity

Push the boundaries of digital journalism through design, interactivity, and multimedia storytelling.

03 — Inclusivity

Ensure every BSCS student has an equal opportunity to publish, contribute, and be heard.

04 — Innovation

Continuously evolve our platform with modern web technologies, AI-assisted tools, and responsive design.

05 — Community

Build a lasting community of student journalists, developers, and creatives united by the love of CS and storytelling.

Foundational Principles

What Guides Every Story We Tell

Truth First

We verify before we publish. Accuracy and integrity are non-negotiable in every piece we produce.

Open Expression

We provide a free, inclusive, and open space where student voices are heard, amplified, and respected.

Tech-Driven

We harness modern web technologies to deliver journalism that is interactive, accessible, and forward-looking.

Timeliness

We deliver relevant, current, and timely coverage of events, innovations, and stories within the BSCS community.

Community First

Every story serves our community. We listen, engage, and create content that matters to our readers.
